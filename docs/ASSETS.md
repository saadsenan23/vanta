# VANTA asset provenance

The campaign visuals were generated specifically for this project with the built-in image generation tool. They are fictional vehicle concepts, not photographs of a manufactured VANTA vehicle. No existing manufacturer logo, third-party automotive model, external runtime image URL, or remote HDR map is used.

## Campaign image briefs

### Exterior

Wide cinematic photoreal studio image of an original, unbranded, dark silver electric grand touring coupe. Front-left three-quarter view facing left, long sculptural hood, narrow horizontal LED headlamp, elegant aerodynamic wheels, graphite studio and concrete floor. Large softbox reflections make the metallic surfaces legible. Car occupies most of the frame with upper negative space for editorial type. No text, logos, neon effects or glowing shapes.

### Interior

Wide cinematic view from the rear passenger position into an original luxury EV cockpit. Charcoal leather and recycled textile concept, brushed metal, restrained pale sage ambient light, sculptural steering wheel, a minimal continuous digital instrument display and stitched seats in the foreground. Soft editorial lighting. No text or logos.
